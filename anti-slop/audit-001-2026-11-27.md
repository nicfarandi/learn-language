# antislop audit 001, 2026-11-27

Scope: every screen in v0.1 (Home, Lesson: Learn cards, exercises, feedback, result; Patch notes) and the app icon.
Contrast was measured with the WCAG formula on the real theme colours.

Pick the numbers to fix. Numbers you don't mention stay as they are.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-02 · em dash in UI text.** The feedback heading reads "Almost — check the spelling".
2. **R-02 · em dash in course content.** Unit 1's example "Ça va ? — Ça va bien, merci." uses the dash
   that French uses to mark a new speaker. Fix: show the two speakers on two lines instead.
3. **R-25 · "done" badge contrast 3.45:1** (white ✓ on green, light theme). Needs 4.5:1.
4. **R-25 · locked lessons 2.45:1** (light) and 3.31:1 (dark): the whole row is faded to 60 %.
5. **R-25 · faded answer options 2.16:1** (light) and 2.97:1 (dark) after you answer: the options you
   didn't pick become hard to read.
6. **R-27 · Home has no loading state.** For a moment it shows "0 of 52 lessons done" and "Start" before
   your progress loads.
7. **R-37 · no design direction.** v0.1 was built without one, so it's a draft. A proposal is in
   `DESIGN.md`, waiting for your OK or changes.
8. **R-23 · app icon and launch screen are Expo's default placeholders.** I won't design one without your
   go-ahead: say what you'd like (e.g. a letter "F" in the notebook style, or your own image).

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

9. **R-04 · emoji used as icons** (🔊 🔒 ✓ ✕). They look different on every phone and weren't chosen for
   the content. Replace with simple drawn icons.
10. **R-06 · uppercase, letter-spaced labels on every screen** ("UP NEXT · UNIT 1", "WHAT DOES THIS
    MEAN?"), used by default.
11. **R-14 · Home is one flat list of 52 identical lesson rows.** No hierarchy: the current unit looks the
    same as finished and far-future ones.

## LOW (Quality Locks)

12. **R-20 / R-29 · no identity.** Default blue and the system font: with the name swapped, it could be
    any app. Fixed by applying `DESIGN.md` (needs 7).
13. **R-11 · the same large radius on every card, option and panel.**
14. **R-31 · no written reasons for colour, layout and type choices.** Fixed by `DESIGN.md` (needs 7).
15. **Screen readers:** lesson rows don't announce "done, best 8 of 10" or "locked".
