# French course plan: zero to an excellent DELF A2

36 units in four stages. Goal: **90+/100 on the DELF A2, every time** (see [GOAL.md](GOAL.md)).

| Stage | Units | Level reached | Words (total) | Time at 30 min/day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Foundations** | 0–12 | First part of A1 | ~350 | ~2 months |
| **2. Complete A1** | 13–18 | A1 | ~600 | +1–2 months |
| **3. A2** | 19–34 | Strong A2 | ~1,800 | +8–10 months |
| **4. DELF A2 prep** | 35 | Exam-ready | — | +2–3 months |

Each lesson: **~5–7 new words + one idea**. A unit ends with a checkpoint (pass mark **90 %**), a stage ends
with a test in the style of the real exam. Grammar is introduced only when the vocabulary to practise it is already known.

**Legend:** 🗣 new words · 💡 the idea explained in the Learn cards

# Stage 1 — Foundations

## Unit 0 — The sounds of French

Mostly listening. Goal: read a French word and guess roughly how it sounds.

| # | Lesson | 💡 Idea | 🗣 Example words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Silent endings | Final letters are usually silent (*petit*, *grand*, *Paris*). Exception: **c, r, f, l** are often said (*avec*, *bonjour*, *neuf*, *il*) | petit, grand, Paris, avec, bonjour |
| 2 | Accents | **é** = "ay", **è / ê** = "eh", plain **e** is soft or silent; **ç** = "s" | café, très, fête, le, garçon |
| 3 | Letter teams | **ou** = "oo", **oi** = "wa", **au / eau** = "oh", **ai** = "eh", **eu** = between "uh" and "ur" | vous, moi, eau, lait, deux |
| 4 | Nasal sounds & linking | **an/en, on, in/ain, un** are said through the nose, no "n" sound. **Liaison**: a silent final consonant is said before a vowel (*vous‿avez*) | enfant, bon, vin, pain, un |

## Unit 1 — Hello

Greet people, give your name, ask how they are. Written out in full in [sample-unit-01.md](sample-unit-01.md).

| # | Lesson | 💡 Idea | 🗣 New words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Greetings | *Bonjour* (polite, anyone) vs *salut* (casual) | bonjour, bonsoir, salut, au revoir, madame, monsieur |
| 2 | Polite words | *s'il vous plaît* (polite) vs *s'il te plaît* (friends) | merci, s'il vous plaît, s'il te plaît, de rien, pardon, excusez-moi |
| 3 | My name is… | *Je m'appelle* = "I call myself" | je m'appelle, comment, tu, vous, enchanté(e), et |
| 4 | How are you? | **tu** vs **vous**: who you say each to | ça va, bien, très, pas mal, et toi, et vous |

## Unit 2 — Who I am (*être*)

| # | Lesson | 💡 Idea | 🗣 New words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | People words | Subject pronouns: je, tu, il, elle, nous, vous, ils, elles; **on** = "we" in everyday talk | je, il, elle, nous, ils, elles, on |
| 2 | To be | *être*: je suis, tu es, il/elle est, nous sommes, vous êtes, ils/elles sont | être, étudiant(e), content(e), fatigué(e) |
| 3 | Nationalities | Adjectives change for a woman: add **-e** (*français → française*). Not capitalised | français(e), anglais(e), américain(e), espagnol(e), australien(ne) |
| 4 | Not | **ne … pas** wraps the verb: *je **ne** suis **pas***; *ne → n'* before a vowel | ne … pas, oui, non, aussi |

## Unit 3 — Things & articles

| # | Lesson | 💡 Idea | 🗣 New words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A / an | Every noun is masculine or feminine: **un** livre, **une** table. Learn the word with its article | un, une, livre, table, chaise, stylo, sac |
| 2 | The | **le** (m), **la** (f), **l'** before a vowel, **les** (plural) | le, la, l', les, ordinateur, porte, téléphone |
| 3 | More than one | Plural: add **-s** (silent!); **des** = "some" for plurals | des, clé, clés, livres, fenêtre |
| 4 | What is it? | *C'est* (it is) / *ce sont* (they are); *Qu'est-ce que c'est ?* | c'est, ce sont, qu'est-ce que c'est, voilà |

## Unit 4 — Food & drink

| # | Lesson | 💡 Idea | 🗣 New words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Food | Recap of le/la/l' with food nouns | pain, fromage, pomme, eau, lait, café, thé |
| 2 | Eat & drink | *je mange* / *je bois*, *tu manges* / *tu bois* | manger, boire, croissant, jus d'orange, vin |
| 3 | Some | **du** (m), **de la** (f), **de l'** (vowel), **des** (plural) = "some"; after *pas* it's just **de**: *pas **de** pain* | du, de la, de l', viande, poisson, légumes |
| 4 | At the café | *Je voudrais…* = polite "I would like"; asking for the bill | je voudrais, un verre, une tasse, l'addition, voici |

## Unit 5 — Numbers

| # | Lesson | 💡 Idea | 🗣 New words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0–10 | Liaison with numbers: *deux‿amis*, *six* said "sees" alone | zéro … dix |
| 2 | 11–20 | 11–16 are their own words; 17–19 are "ten-seven" (*dix-sept*) | onze … vingt |
| 3 | 21–69 | *vingt **et** un*, then *vingt-deux*…; tens: trente, quarante, cinquante, soixante | trente, quarante, cinquante, soixante |
| 4 | Age & price | Age uses **avoir** (have), not être: *j'**ai** 25 ans*; *Ça coûte combien ?* | j'ai … ans, quel âge, combien, euro |

## Unit 6 — Family (*avoir*)

| # | Lesson | 💡 Idea | 🗣 New words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | To have | *avoir*: j'ai, tu as, il a, nous avons, vous avez, ils ont | avoir, un chien, un chat, une voiture |
| 2 | Family | Recap of un/une, le/la with people | mère, père, frère, sœur, parents, enfant, ami(e) |
| 3 | My, your, his/her | **mon / ma / mes**, ton/ta/tes, son/sa/ses. Agrees with the **thing owned**, not the owner. *mon amie* before a vowel | mon, ma, mes, ton, ta, tes, son, sa, ses |
| 4 | Describing people | Adjectives go **after** the noun, except short common ones (beauty, age, good, size) go before: *un **petit** chat* | grand(e), petit(e), jeune, vieux/vieille, gentil(le), beau/belle |

## Unit 7 — Everyday verbs (-er)

| # | Lesson | 💡 Idea | 🗣 New words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | To speak | Regular **-er** verbs: drop -er, add -e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent. **-e, -es, -ent all sound the same** | parler, français, anglais |
| 2 | More verbs | Same pattern; **je → j'** before a vowel (*j'habite*) | habiter, travailler, aimer, écouter, regarder |
| 3 | Likes | With *aimer*, use **le/la/les**: *j'aime **le** café* (coffee in general) | adorer, détester, la musique, le sport, les films |
| 4 | Questions | Three ways: raise your voice, **est-ce que**, or swap (*parlez-vous*). Start with the first two | est-ce que, où, quand, pourquoi, parce que |

## Unit 8 — Time & days

| # | Lesson | 💡 Idea | 🗣 New words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Days | Days are not capitalised; *le lundi* = every Monday | lundi … dimanche, aujourd'hui, demain, hier |
| 2 | Dates | *le 3 mars*, but *le **premier** mai*; months not capitalised | janvier … décembre, le premier |
| 3 | The time | *Il est trois heures*; et quart, et demie, moins le quart; midi, minuit | heure, quart, demie, midi, minuit |
| 4 | My day | Parts of the day; *le matin* = in the morning | le matin, l'après-midi, le soir, la nuit, tôt, tard |

## Unit 9 — Places (*aller*)

| # | Lesson | 💡 Idea | 🗣 New words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Places | Recap of le/la/l' with places | maison, école, travail, gare, supermarché, parc, ville |
| 2 | To go | *aller*: je vais, tu vas, il va, nous allons, vous allez, ils vont. **à + le = au**, **à + les = aux** | aller, au, à la, à l', aux |
| 3 | Going to… | Near future: *aller* + verb = "going to": *je **vais manger*** | ce soir, ce week-end, bientôt |
| 4 | Directions | *Où est… ?*; asking and giving simple directions | à gauche, à droite, tout droit, près, loin |

## Unit 10 — Home

| # | Lesson | 💡 Idea | 🗣 New words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rooms | Recap of plurals with rooms | cuisine, chambre, salle de bains, salon, jardin |
| 2 | There is | **il y a** = there is / there are; *il n'y a **pas de*** | il y a, lit, canapé, lampe, frigo |
| 3 | Colours | Colours go after the noun and agree: *une table **blanche*** | blanc(he), noir(e), rouge, bleu(e), vert(e) |
| 4 | Where things are | Position words | sur, sous, dans, devant, derrière, à côté de |

## Unit 11 — Weather & free time (*faire*)

| # | Lesson | 💡 Idea | 🗣 New words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | To do / make | *faire*: je fais, tu fais, il fait, nous faisons, vous faites, ils font | faire, les courses, la cuisine |
| 2 | Weather | Weather uses **il fait** (*il fait beau*), except rain and snow: *il pleut*, *il neige* | beau, chaud, froid, il pleut, il neige |
| 3 | Hobbies | *faire **du** sport*; *jouer **au** foot* (games); *jouer **de la** guitare* (instruments) | jouer, foot, tennis, guitare, piano, lire |
| 4 | How often | **ne … jamais** = never, wraps the verb like ne … pas | toujours, souvent, parfois, jamais |

## Unit 35 — The exam

| # | Block | Content |

## What the app adds beyond the basic lessons

- **Story lessons:** a short text shown and spoken line by line, tap any word for its meaning, then questions.
- **Real-life documents:** emails, ads, timetables, notices and announcements, like the ones in the exam.
- **Dictation:** hear a sentence, type it.
- **Listening at exam level:** normal speed, several voices, from Stage 3.
- **Free writing:** 60–80 word texts, marked per criterion with corrections.
- **Speaking:** record an answer, which is transcribed and marked, with corrections and a model answer.
- **Grammar review:** rules come back in the review deck, not only words.

## Speaking: the one part the app can't fully cover

The app marks *what* you say and how correct it is, but it can't fully judge pronunciation or how naturally
you speak. From Stage 3, add a real person, such as a tutor or a language-exchange partner, once every week or
two. Before the exam, do at least one full mock speaking test with them.

## Writing rules for the content

- English explanations: short, plain, no grammar jargon without an example right after it.
- French uses standard spelling with accents; masculine/feminine forms shown as *content(e)*.
- Every noun is taught with its article.
- Every sentence in a lesson uses only words from that lesson or earlier ones.
- You review each unit's French before it ships.
